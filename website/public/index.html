Inside rtxForge

Neural rendering.
Across your library.

Select your games and deploy NR in one pass, on compatible RTX cards.

Multi-frame generation.
RTX 20, 30 and 40 series.

Deploy MFG across your compatible games, then tune each game’s preset.

Choose your
packages.

Choose a supported package or inspect your own ZIP. Pick NR, MFG or both for compatible games.

Tune presets.

Set NR strength, sharpening and frame-generation presets. Use library defaults, then fine-tune individual games.

Install.

Deploy NR and MFG across your selected games. Your original files stay backed up for restore.

Play.

Open your library in Game Mode, with larger artwork and controller navigation.

Game and GPU support depends on the installed provider. Neural rendering is experimental.

Before you install+

Linux x86_64 with a working NVIDIA driver. Package-specific requirements are listed with each release.

NR works with compatible RTX cards. MFG supports RTX 20, 30 and 40 series. Check compatibility for your game and installed provider. Neural rendering is experimental.

Start with one game. Close it before making changes, review the files, and keep the original-file backups. Avoid protected online games and anti-cheat.

Found a bug?+

Open Settings → Recovery → Library Reports → Preview Support Report in rtxForge. Review the report, export it, then attach it to your bug report.

Include your platform, GPU and driver, game, provider, and what happened. Logs and notes are optional. Check the export before sharing; redaction can miss personal information.
