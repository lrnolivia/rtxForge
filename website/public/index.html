Before you install+

Linux x86_64 with a working NVIDIA driver, Python 3, GTK4, libadwaita 1.7+, and PyGObject. The AppImage checks capabilities, so it isn't limited to Bazzite.

Feature installs currently target RTX 40-series cards and compatible x64 games. Other GPU generations are unvalidated. NR is experimental; results vary by game.

Start with one game. Close it before making changes, review the files, and keep the original-file backups. Avoid protected online games and anti-cheat.

Found a bug?+

Open Settings → Recovery → Library Reports → Preview Support Report in rtxForge. Review the report, export it, then attach it to your bug report.

Include your platform, GPU and driver, game, provider, and what happened. Logs and notes are optional. Check the export before sharing; redaction can miss personal information.
